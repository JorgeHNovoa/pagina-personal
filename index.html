About

Hi, I'm Jorge Novoa. I hold a M.Sc. in Applied Mathematics, as well as a degree in Mathematical Engineering, both from Universidad de Chile. I am currently interested in pursuing a PhD in Mathematics, with a strong focus on differential equations. I hope to pursue doctoral opportunities that will allow me to further deepen my understanding of this field, as well as of nonlinear analysis.

Research Interests

  • Nonlinear Analysis (particularly fixed-point theorems, topological degree theory, variational methods and monotone operator theory)
  • Differential Equations (focusing on existence and multiplicity of solutions, and boundary value problems)
